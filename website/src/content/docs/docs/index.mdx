OpenBuddies is a free, open-source, self-hosted way to give your children AI safely. It runs on a computer in your home.
Your children make their own AI buddies to chat, draw, take quizzes and get homework help, and you can read every
message and set the rules.

<DocsWelcome />

These docs are split into four kinds of page, so you can find what you need quickly. (It's the
[Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr) way of writing docs.) There's also a section of its own for writing your own apps.
