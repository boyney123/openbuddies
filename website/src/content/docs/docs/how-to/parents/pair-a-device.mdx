## Pair a device

<Steps>

1. Make a fresh pairing code for the child:

   ```sh
   npx openbuddies pair <child>
   ```

2. Scan the QR code with the child's device. Each code works once and expires after 10 minutes.

</Steps>

The device needs to be on your home Wi-Fi, unless you've set up [access away from home](/docs/how-to/parents/use-away-from-home/).

## Add it to the home screen

On Chrome or Edge over HTTPS, the pairing page shows an **Add to my home screen** button. On an iPhone or iPad, tap
**Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. You'll need HTTPS (from Tailscale) for reminder notifications on the device.

## Unpair a device

Open your parent page and go to **Devices**, then tap **Revoke** beside the device. It's cut off straight away:
anything it has open stops working at once. You can also pair a new device from here with **Pair a new device**.
