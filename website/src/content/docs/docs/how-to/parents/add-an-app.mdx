Homework help, Picture Studio and Quizzes come built in and are on for every child. Apps you add start switched off,
so nothing reaches a child until you choose.

Whichever way you add one, OpenBuddies shows you the whole app first (what it uses, and every word of its
instructions) and asks before adding it. The copy installed is exactly the one you read.

## On your parent page

Open **Apps** to see every app and tick which children get it. Below them is the gallery: tap **Look at it** to read an
app before adding it. You can also add a catalogue, or an app someone sent you a link to.

## Other ways to add an app

### From the gallery

See what's there, then add one by its id:

```sh
openbuddies apps gallery
openbuddies apps add spelling-bee
```

The gallery includes **Spelling Bee**, **Times Tables** and **Story Planner**.

### From a link

Someone may share an app as a single `.json` file at an `https://` link:

```sh
openbuddies apps add https://example.com/times-tables.json
```

Or on your parent page: **Apps → Add an app by link**.

### From a catalogue

A catalogue is a list of apps someone keeps, like a teacher's class apps. Add its link on your parent page under
**Apps → Catalogues**, and its apps appear in your gallery.

## Turn it on for a child

```sh
openbuddies apps on <child> spelling-bee
openbuddies apps off <child> spelling-bee
```

Or switch it on and off under **Apps** on your parent page. To remove an app completely, run
`openbuddies apps remove <app>`.
