Without extra setup, children's devices only work on your home Wi-Fi, and your parent page only opens on the home
computer. [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) gives you a private HTTPS address that only your own devices can reach.

<Steps>

1. On the home computer, run:

   ```sh
   npx openbuddies tailscale setup
   ```

   It asks before installing anything. It then signs you in (it shows a QR code, so a computer with no screen can be
   signed in from your phone) and turns on Tailscale Serve.

2. Install the Tailscale app on each phone and tablet, and sign in with the same account. OpenBuddies shows a QR code
   for the download.

3. Make new pairing codes with `npx openbuddies pair <child>`. They now use the HTTPS address.

</Steps>

HTTPS also lets devices add OpenBuddies to the home screen properly and turn on reminder notifications.

Check it with `npx openbuddies tailscale status`, and turn it off with `npx openbuddies tailscale off`.
