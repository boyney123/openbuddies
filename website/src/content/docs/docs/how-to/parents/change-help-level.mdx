Each child has one help level, which every subject follows unless you give a subject its own.

| Level | What the buddy does |
|---|---|
| **Strict** | Never gives homework answers. Questions, hints and worked examples of similar problems only. |
| **Balanced** | Shows the answer after real effort. |
| **Relaxed** | Explains freely. |

## Change a child's help level

```sh
npx openbuddies children mode <child> strict
```

Or open your parent page, go to **Children**, and pick a level.

## Give one subject its own level

```sh
npx openbuddies subjects add <child> history --mode relaxed
npx openbuddies subjects mode <child> maths strict
```

Children can add subjects themselves when they make a buddy. A new subject starts as **Strict**, and you can change
or remove it under **Children**. See every subject with `npx openbuddies subjects list <child>`.

To understand how the levels work turn by turn, read [Help levels and the hint ladder](/docs/explanation/help-levels/).
