You can read every message your child sends and every reply their buddy gives.

## See how a child is getting on

On your parent page, open **Sessions** and choose a child. Their page shows:

- how much of **today's AI allowance** they've used
- their sessions, time learning, problems solved and messages
- any **alerts**
- their **buddies**, and what each one is best at
- the **reminders and plans** they've set up with their buddies
- **all their sessions**, newest first. **Live** means the chat is still going.

## Read a chat

Open a session to read it. At the top is a summary: how long it lasted, problems solved, hints given and answers
checked.

**Problems worked on** shows each problem, and how far up the help ladder the buddy went before your child got it:
**Ask**, **Nudge**, **Worked example** or **Check**. See [Help levels and the hint ladder](/docs/explanation/help-levels/).

<Screenshots device="desktop" shots={[{ src: session, alt: "A session summary and the problems worked on" }]} />

## See why the buddy said what it said

The conversation has two columns:

- **What your child saw**: the chat, exactly as it appeared on their screen.
- **Behind the scenes**: for each message, what OpenBuddies made of it (for example "stuck", the subject and the help
  level), and what the buddy did next, like giving a hint or checking an answer.

Behind the scenes also shows any alert, next to the message that raised it, and any reply the buddy drafted that gave
too much away and was blocked before your child saw it.
