Your parent page is where you see what your children have been doing with their buddies, and set the rules.

## Open it

`npx openbuddies start` opens it for you. On the home computer, you can also go to
[localhost:4747/parent](http://localhost:4747/parent) (or the port you chose during setup). To open it from your phone
when you're out, see [Use it away from home](/docs/how-to/parents/use-away-from-home/).

Enter your parent PIN to unlock it.

## What's on it

- **Sessions** is where you start. Each child has a card showing their sessions this week, problems solved and time
  spent learning, with their latest chats underneath. Open a child or a chat to read it:
  [Read your child's chats](/docs/how-to/parents/read-your-childs-chats/).
- **Alerts** lists anything worrying a child has said, with a count while any are unread:
  [Check alerts](/docs/how-to/parents/get-alerts/).
- **Children** is where you set each child's rules: their [help level](/docs/how-to/parents/change-help-level/), their
  [daily AI allowance](/docs/how-to/parents/set-daily-allowance/), what they can look up online, and their subjects.
  You can [add a child](/docs/how-to/parents/add-a-child/) here too.
- **Apps** shows the apps on each child's home screen, and the gallery of apps you can add:
  [Add an app](/docs/how-to/parents/add-an-app/).
- **Devices** shows each child's paired phones and tablets. Pair a new one, or revoke one that's lost:
  [Pair a phone or tablet](/docs/how-to/parents/pair-a-device/).

When you're done, **Log out** is at the bottom of the menu.
