---
title: "Add a child"
description: "Add another child to your household, with their own help level."
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

Run:

```sh
openbuddies children add
```

It asks the same questions as the setup: their first name, age, school year, and how much help they get (**Strict**,
**Balanced** or **Relaxed**). To skip the questions, pass the answers:

```sh
openbuddies children add --name Mia --age 8 --year "Year 3" --mode balanced
```

Then pair their device: see [Pair a phone or tablet](/docs/how-to/parents/pair-a-device/).

You can also add and edit children on your parent page under **Children**. To see who's set up, run
`openbuddies children list`.
