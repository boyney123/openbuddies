If a child says something worrying, OpenBuddies raises an alert **before** the buddy replies. Alerts are under
**Alerts** on your parent page, with a count beside it while any are unread.

An alert is a short summary, never your child's own words. Open it to read the chat it came from.

<Screenshots device="desktop" shots={[{ src: alerts, alt: "An alert: Sam seems frustrated or upset during Chat. You may want to check in." }]} />

OpenBuddies doesn't send alerts to your phone yet, so check your parent page now and then.

How readily worrying messages raise an alert is set for each child with `alerts.welfare.sensitivity` in their
`rules.yaml`: `high` (the default) also alerts you when they seem upset, while `medium` and `low` alert you only when
something sounds worrying.
