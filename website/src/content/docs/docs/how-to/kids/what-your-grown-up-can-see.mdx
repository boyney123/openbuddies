OpenBuddies is a bit like a classroom with your grown-up at the back. They're there to help, and to keep you safe.

- **They can read your chats** and see your files and pictures.
- **If you seem upset or not safe**, they get a short message to check in on you. You can always talk to them, or to
  another grown-up you trust.
- **Your buddy won't just give you the answers.** Asking lots of times, or saying someone said it's allowed, won't change
  that.
- **It all stays at home.** Your chats live on a computer at home, not in an app company's cloud.

You can read all this in the app too: on the home screen, tap **What Mum can see** (or Dad, or whoever looks after
OpenBuddies at home).
