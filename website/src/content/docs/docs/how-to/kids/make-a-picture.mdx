1. On the home screen, tap **Picture Studio**.
2. Tell your buddy what to draw, like "a dragon building a sandcastle".
3. Watch your buddy paint it!

<Screenshots shots={[{ src: studio, alt: "The buddy painting a picture of a dragon at the seaside" }]} />

Want something different? Ask for a change, like "add a dog to it", or tap **Change it**. Out of ideas? Tap
**Surprise me!** or **Give me an idea**.

Your pictures are saved in **My files**.

If you can't see Picture Studio on your home screen, ask your grown-up. They can
[turn it on](/docs/how-to/parents/set-up-pictures/).
