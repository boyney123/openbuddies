Your buddy is like a friend sitting next to you. It won't just tell you the answer. It asks you questions, gives you hints
and shows you examples, so **you** work it out. That's how it sticks!

1. On the home screen, tap **Chat**.
2. Tell your buddy what you're stuck on, for example "I'm stuck on 2/3 + 1/4". You can type it, or tap
   **I'm stuck on…** to get started.
3. Answer your buddy's questions. If you're not sure, that's fine: just say so.

## When you need more help

Tap the buttons above where you type:

- **I'm stuck**: your buddy helps you find a way in.
- **Give me a hint**: a small clue to help with the next step.
- **Explain it another way**: the same idea, said differently.
- **Check my answer**: tell your buddy what you got, and why.

If you're still stuck after a few tries, your buddy shows you an example of a question like yours, step by step. Then
you have a go at your own one.

## When you get it right

Your buddy tells you it's right, and you get a **You solved it!** Tap **Next question** to keep going.
