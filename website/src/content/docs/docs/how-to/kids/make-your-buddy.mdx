The first time you open OpenBuddies, you make your study buddy. It's yours, so make it however you like!

1. **Give it a name.** Type one, or tap the dice for a random name.
2. **Say who it is**, if you want to. For example: "A friendly maths wizard who loves puzzles".
3. **Pick what it's best at**, like Maths or English. You can leave this as **Any subject**.
4. **Tap the brush** to choose its colour and something to wear: a hat, glasses, a scarf and more. Tap **Done** when it
   looks just right.
5. **Tap Meet** and your buddy hatches!

## Change how it looks later

On the home screen, tap the brush next to your buddy. You can change its colour and outfit whenever you like.

## Make more buddies

Tap **Buddies** at the bottom of the screen to make another one, or to switch between them. You can have up to 10.
