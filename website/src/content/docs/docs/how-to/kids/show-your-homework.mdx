Typing out a whole question is slow. Show your buddy a photo of it instead!

## In the chat

1. Tap the paper clip next to where you type.
2. Take a photo of your homework, or choose a file.
3. Tell your buddy which question you're on.

## In My files

Everything you share is kept in **My files**, on the home screen. You can take a photo or choose a file there too.

Later, just mention it in the chat, like "check my history homework", and your buddy finds it.
