1. In the chat, ask your buddy for a quiz, like "make me a quiz about fractions".
2. Pick **Multiple choice** or **Type answers**.
3. When it's ready, tap **Take it**.

All your quizzes are in **Quizzes** on the home screen, so you can take them again whenever you like. You can ask for a
new one there too.
