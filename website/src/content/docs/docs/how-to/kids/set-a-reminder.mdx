1. In the chat, ask your buddy, like "remind me tomorrow at 8 to pack my PE kit".
2. Check the time is right, then tap **Set it**.

<Screenshots shots={[{ src: reminder, alt: "A reminder to drink some water, with Set it and No thanks buttons" }]} />

To see your reminders, tap the alarm clock at the top of the home screen. You can pause or delete them there, and turn
on notifications so your buddy can remind you even when the app is closed.

On an iPhone or iPad, add OpenBuddies to your Home Screen first: tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and open it
from there.
