In this tutorial you'll install OpenBuddies on a computer at home, set up your household, and pair your child's phone.
By the end, your child will have hatched their first buddy and you'll be reading their first chat on your parent page.

It takes about 15 minutes.

## What you'll need

- **A computer that stays at home**, running macOS or Linux. An old laptop is fine. Your child never needs to touch it.
- **An AI key** from [Anthropic](https://console.anthropic.com) or [OpenAI](https://platform.openai.com), which
  OpenBuddies is tuned for, or another provider such as Google or OpenRouter. You pay them
  for what your family uses.
- **Your child's phone, tablet or laptop**, on the same Wi-Fi as the computer.

<BuddyAside type="caution" title="Read your AI provider's rules first">
  AI providers have rules for products used by people under 18. Read yours, for example the
  [Anthropic Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) or the
  [OpenAI Usage Policies](https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies), before your child starts.
</BuddyAside>

## Install OpenBuddies

<Steps>

1. On the home computer, open **Terminal** (on a Mac, press Cmd+Space and type "Terminal").

2. Paste this and press Enter:

   ```sh
   curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/boyney123/openbuddies/main/install.sh | bash
   ```

   It installs OpenBuddies in a folder of its own (`~/.openbuddies`), along with Node.js if the computer needs it, then
   starts the setup below. Nothing needs your computer password. Later, `openbuddies update` moves you to the newest version.

   Already use Node.js 22 or newer? You can install with `npm install -g openbuddies` instead, then run
   `openbuddies init`.

</Steps>

## Set up your household

<Steps>

1. The installer starts the setup for you. (If you installed with npm, or closed it, run `openbuddies init`.)

2. Tell it about you: your name, what your child calls you (say, "Dad"), and a parent PIN of 4 to 8 digits. The PIN
   protects your parent page.

3. Choose your AI provider (**Anthropic** or **OpenAI** are recommended; **Another provider** lists the rest), keep
   the suggested models unless you know you want others, and paste your AI key. OpenBuddies checks the key works before
   going on. It's saved in the household folder and never leaves this computer.

4. Add your child: their first name, age and school year, and how much help OpenBuddies should give them:

   - **Strict**: never gives homework answers.
   - **Balanced**: shows the answer after real effort.
   - **Relaxed**: explains freely.

   Pick **Strict** if you're not sure. You can change it any time. Add more children if you have them.

5. Say yes to starting OpenBuddies automatically when the computer starts.

</Steps>

You've now got a **household folder**, `~/openbuddies`: everything OpenBuddies knows about your family lives there.
Copy it to back everything up. (The program itself is in `~/.openbuddies`, which you never need to open. To keep the
household somewhere else, set up with `openbuddies init --home <folder>`.)

## Start it and pair your child's phone

<Steps>

1. When the setup finishes, it starts OpenBuddies for you: your parent page opens in the browser, and the terminal shows
   a QR code for each child. (Any time later, `openbuddies start` does the same.)

2. On your child's phone, open the camera and scan their QR code. The code works once and expires in 10 minutes. If it
   runs out, make a new one with `openbuddies pair <child>`.

3. Hand the phone over. Your child hatches their first buddy: they give it a name and a colour, and can say what it's
   like ("a funny pirate") and what it's best at.

4. Let them say hello and ask their buddy something.

</Steps>

## Read the chat

Go back to your parent page, enter your PIN, and open **Sessions**. You'll see the chat your child just had, every
message, with notes on what the buddy decided at each turn. To find your way around, see
[Find your way around your parent page](/docs/how-to/parents/your-parent-page/) and
[Read your child's chats](/docs/how-to/parents/read-your-childs-chats/).

That's it: your family is set up.
