Everything on your child's home screen is an app, and an app is just two text files: one says what it is, the other
tells the buddy how to help. In this tutorial you'll write a quick-fire Times Tables app, try it, and turn it on for
your child.

You'll need a household that's already set up. If you haven't done that yet, follow
[Set up OpenBuddies for your family](/docs/tutorials/set-up-your-household/) first.

## Make the folder

Apps you add live in the `apps` folder inside your household folder. The folder's name is the app's id.

<Steps>

1. Make a folder for the app:

   ```sh
   mkdir -p ~/openbuddies/apps/times-tables
   ```

2. You'll end up with this:

   <FileTree>
   - openbuddies/
     - apps/
       - times-tables/
         - app.yaml what it is and what it uses
         - SKILL.md how the buddy helps
   </FileTree>

</Steps>

## Say what the app is

Create `~/openbuddies/apps/times-tables/app.yaml`:

```yaml
name: Times Tables
description: Quick-fire times tables.
art: chart
icon: brain
version: 1.0.0
author: Your name
kind: tutor
needs: [reminders]
```

That gives the app a name and a line under it on the home screen, picks a drawing for its card, and makes it a chat
with the buddy (`kind: tutor`). `needs: [reminders]` lets your child ask for a reminder to practise.

## Tell the buddy how to help

Create `~/openbuddies/apps/times-tables/SKILL.md`:

```md
# Times Tables

## Role
You help the child practise their times tables. Ask which table they want to practise, or offer to mix them up.

## How to practise
- One question at a time, like "What's 7 × 8?".
- If they're right, cheer briefly and ask the next one.
- If not, give a tip (7 × 8 is 7 × 4, doubled) and let them try again before showing the answer.
- After ten questions, tell them their score and offer to practise the tricky ones.

## Keep it short
One or two short sentences each time. Make it feel like a game.
```

You don't need to repeat OpenBuddies' safety rules here: they always sit above your instructions.

## Turn it on and try it

<Steps>

1. OpenBuddies spots the new folder straight away. Turn the app on for your child:

   ```sh
   npx openbuddies apps on <child> times-tables
   ```

   Or open your parent page, go to **Apps**, and switch it on for them there.

2. On your child's phone, the **Times Tables** card is now on the home screen. Open it and say "let's do the 7s".

3. Back on your parent page, open **Sessions** to read how it went.

</Steps>

Well done: you've written an app. To give it to other families, see [Share an app](/docs/how-to/parents/share-an-app/), and for
every field and option, see the [app format reference](/docs/reference/app-format/).
